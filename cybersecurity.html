01 / WHAT THIS FIELD IS

Cybersecurity & Pentesting in plain terms

So you know what you are walking into — and what you are not.

What people in this field actually do
  • Figure out how systems can fail or be abused
  • Design controls so damage is limited when something goes wrong
  • Test applications and networks (with permission) to find weaknesses
  • Investigate incidents and recover from them
  • Write clear reports so others can fix what was found
Common entry paths
  • Defence / blue team — monitoring, hardening, response
  • Offence / pentest — authorised testing and proof of risk
  • AppSec — secure design and code review
  • Forensics / IR — what happened and how to recover
  • Governance — policy, risk, and compliance support
Mindset that matters

Scope before you test. Assume the browser can be inspected. Validate on the server. Prefer evidence over guesswork. Stay legal: only systems you own or are explicitly allowed to test. The lab on this site is a simulation — it never connects to real targets.
