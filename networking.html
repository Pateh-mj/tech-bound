01 / WHAT THIS FIELD IS

Networking & Infrastructure in plain terms

So you know what you are walking into — and what you are not.

What people in this field actually do
  • Design and maintain reliable connectivity between systems and people
  • Configure routers, switches, firewalls, and wireless access
  • Troubleshoot latency, packet loss, and path problems
  • Plan IP addressing, VLANs, and segmentation
  • Monitor traffic and keep networks available under load
Common entry paths
  • Network engineering — design, implementation, and operations
  • NetOps / NOC — monitoring, incidents, and uptime
  • Wireless — coverage, capacity, and RF basics
  • Cloud networking — VPCs, gateways, hybrid links
  • Network security — segmentation and perimeter controls
Mindset that matters

Start from the path of the packet. Draw the topology before you change anything. Measure before and after. Prefer simple, documented designs over clever ones nobody can debug at 2 a.m.
