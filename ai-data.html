01 / WHAT THIS FIELD IS

AI, Data & Automation in plain terms

So you know what you are walking into — and what you are not.

What people in this field actually do
  • Collect and clean data so questions can be answered honestly
  • Build models or rules that support decisions — not replace judgment blindly
  • Automate repetitive work with scripts and pipelines
  • Evaluate results against reality, not only training metrics
  • Explain limits and risks when AI is involved
Common entry paths
  • Data analysis — metrics, dashboards, storytelling
  • ML engineering — training, serving, monitoring models
  • Automation — scripts, agents, workflow tools
  • Data engineering — pipelines and reliable storage
  • Applied AI product — features that use models carefully
Mindset that matters

Garbage in, confident nonsense out. Start with the decision you want to improve. Prefer simple baselines before complex models. Always ask what happens when the model is wrong.
