01 / WHAT THIS FIELD IS

Frontend & UI/UX in plain terms

So you know what you are walking into — and what you are not.

What people in this field actually do
  • Design screens and flows that people can complete without friction
  • Implement interfaces in the browser with care for performance
  • Make experiences usable with keyboard, screen readers, and varied devices
  • Prototype quickly and test with real users when possible
  • Collaborate with developers and product thinkers on trade-offs
Common entry paths
  • UI engineering — component systems and front-end code
  • Product design — flows, wireframes, research
  • Accessibility — inclusive experiences by default
  • Design systems — shared patterns across a product
  • Motion / interaction — feedback that guides without distracting
Mindset that matters

Clarity beats decoration. Design for the worst connection and the smallest screen you support. If a user has to guess, the interface failed — not the user.
